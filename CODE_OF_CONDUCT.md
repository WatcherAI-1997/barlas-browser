# Code of Conduct

BARLAS Browser welcomes contributors of every background, language, culture,
and level of experience.

## Our standards

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, give actionable feedback, and
assume good intent while remaining precise about technical risks. Harassment,
threats, hate speech, doxxing, discrimination, and deliberate disruption are
not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior harms the community. Serious safety concerns should be reported
privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels.

This project exists to build useful technology through open, constructive
collaboration.
